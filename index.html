Who We Are

About Us

ThermoDynamics (FTC Team 28874) was founded in 2025. We combine computer science, mechanical design, and strategy to build competitive robots. We value teamwork and sportsmanship, and strive to become the best we can be.

🤝

Our Philosophy

We strive to become better teammates through collaboration, teamwork, and coordination.

💪

Teamwork

Each of our members has strong bonds and strives to make them stronger every season.

🏆

Competition Goals

We strive to earn at least 150 points consistently and show good sportsmanship.

⚙️

Innovation

Every season we think differently, build boldly, and grow stronger together.

Our Backbone

The Apex Maker Club

ThermoDynamics is proud to be powered by the Apex Maker Club — a vibrant, youth-led community dedicated to making STEM experiential learning accessible to all.

01

Learn

Focuses on creative computational thinking and building a 'maker mindset' that empowers students to tackle real-world problems.

02

Make

Encourages members to dream, design, and take projects from concept to reality — prototype, iterate, and ship.

03

Share

Builds community by sharing projects and receiving constructive feedback — fostering growth through collaboration.

The Apex Maker Club provides the necessary resources, mentorship, and collaborative spirit that allows our FTC team to thrive.
